Custom software.

Instead of bending your workflow around standard software, we program a tool that fits your workflow.

What we build for you.

Consulting and concept
Together with you, we clarify who will use the application, which tasks it should make easier and which functions are needed for that.
Design and prototypes
We design user interfaces and use a clickable mock-up to test whether the operation is easy to understand.
Custom software development
We develop applications for specific business processes, for example for job scheduling, order management or quote creation.
App development
We develop mobile apps for iOS and Android, for example for appointment booking or for documenting work on site.
Web development
We build company websites, online shops and web applications such as customer portals or booking systems.
Integration and data transfer
We connect the new solution to your existing systems and transfer existing data.
Testing, rollout and maintenance
We test the functions, release the solution and, after launch, fix bugs, install updates and add features.

When it pays off.

  • When standard solutions are no longer enough.
  • When you constantly work around the limits of a piece of software.
  • When your website should fit your audience and no template can do that.

How we work with you.

  1. 1

    Conversation

    You tell us what the tool or website should do. We keep asking until we have understood.

  2. 2

    Analysis

    We look at how it works today and what you already have.

  3. 3

    Strategy

    We decide what is built first, what later and what not at all.

  4. 4

    Implementation

    We program it ourselves and adjust where needed. Depending on the project, we work with suitable specialists.
