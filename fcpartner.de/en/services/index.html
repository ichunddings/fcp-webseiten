What we do.

Marketing is at the center. IT consulting and software come on top when you need them. How much of it, we work out with you.

  • What do you stand for, who do you want to reach and through which channels? We put that in order and keep developing it with you.

  • We look at processes, systems, data and tools, and at what your company actually needs of them.

  • A plan that says what happens first, what can wait and what you should leave alone.

  • Who buys from you, why, and where do you find those people? We settle that before any money is spent.

  • A route by which prospects get in touch with you, and one you can trace: where they came from and what became of them.

  • We plan your digital campaigns, run them, check the numbers and change what is not working.

  • We select suitable creators, approach them and coordinate the collaboration.

  • We look for the routines that eat up time and introduce tools or automations that simplify them.

  • Instead of bending your workflow around standard software, we program a tool that fits your workflow.

  • We design and program your website ourselves, to fit your audience.
