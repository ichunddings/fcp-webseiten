IT consulting from Berlin.

We look at processes, systems, data and tools, and at what your company actually needs of them.

What we do for you.

IT strategy
We assess your existing systems and develop a plan: which investments make sense and what should be done first.
Software selection and introduction
We clarify the requirements with your departments, compare suitable solutions and support the introduction.
Process automation
We identify recurring manual work and simplify processes, for example by having orders transferred to accounting automatically. It usually starts with a simple question: what do you do by hand every week that does not have to be done by hand?
Cloud and infrastructure
We plan servers, networks and workstations and support your company with the move to the cloud.
IT security
We assess risks and develop concepts for access rights, data backup and recovery after outages.
Project management and training
We coordinate internal teams and external providers and train your employees in using new systems.

For individual specialist topics, such as IT security, we work with partner companies that specialize in them.

When this makes sense.

  • When standard solutions are no longer enough.
  • When marketing and IT come together and nobody has the whole picture.
  • When you know something in your process is stuck, but not why.

How we work with you.

  1. 1

    Conversation

    You tell us how things work today and what bothers you. We keep asking until we have understood.

  2. 2

    Analysis

    We look at the processes, systems, data and tools you already have.

  3. 3

    Strategy

    We decide what is changed first, what later and what not at all.

  4. 4

    Implementation

    If you like, we introduce it with you and adjust where needed. Depending on the project, we work with suitable specialists.

And if a tool is missing?

If there is nothing suitable for your process, we program a tool ourselves. More under custom software. If your questions are more about customers and channels, you will find the answers under marketing consulting.
