How we work.

Fischer Consulting Partner is based in Berlin. We have over ten years of experience in marketing, also advise on IT solutions and program what is missing ourselves. We work for individuals just as much as for large companies.

No budget without a plan

Advice and delivery

We advise on marketing and IT. If you like, we carry out what was decided with you, all the way to programming. Depending on the project, we work with suitable specialists.
