Frequently asked questions.

Short answers to what we are asked most often before a first conversation.

Who do you work for?

For individuals, the self-employed, small and mid-sized businesses and large companies. Someone working alone needs something different from a corporation, and we adapt to that.

Do you have references?

Yes. Many of our clients want to remain confidential, so we do not name every client. Most are mid-sized companies, and the software is often only meant for internal use. But we have also worked with large companies.

Do you only work in Berlin?

No. We are based in Berlin and work for clients throughout Germany and internationally.

Do I have to book marketing, IT consulting and software together?

No. All three are available separately, and you can combine them as you like. How much of it, we work out with you.

Do you only advise, or do you also carry things out?

Both. We advise on marketing and IT. If you like, we carry out what was decided with you, all the way to programming. Depending on the project, we work with suitable specialists and with partner companies that specialize in individual topics.

Do you program yourselves?

Yes. We program apps, tools and websites ourselves when standard software does not fit your workflow.

Do you also develop mobile apps?

Yes, for iOS and Android, for example for appointment booking or for documenting work on site.

Do you look after the software after launch?

Yes. We test the functions, release the solution and, after launch, fix bugs, install updates and add features.

I do not know exactly what I need yet. Is that a problem?

No, that is what the first conversation is for. You tell us what it is about, and we keep asking until we have understood. It always starts with the same three questions: What do you want to achieve? Who do you want to reach? How much time and money can you put in?

What does lead generation mean for you?

A way for prospects to get in touch with you that you can trace: where they came from and what became of them.

What happens after my inquiry?

We read your message and get back to you by email or phone. Then we work out together whether and how we can help.

What happens to the data from the contact form?

The form transmits your details encrypted to our server, and from there your inquiry reaches us by email. On the website itself they are not stored in a database. Everything else is in the privacy policy.

Is your question not listed? Tell us what it is about. We will get back to you.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us briefly what it is about. We will get back to you.
